Becoming a Better Geographer

12/5/2021 By: Z.A

Let's face it: Not all of us are that good at geography. I mean, that's understandble right? It's a HUGE subject: Land masses, countries, not to mention stuff INSIDE the countries.
So, the million (Maybe even billion) dollar question is this: How do I get better?
Well, duh, I don't know for sure, but we DO have some tips.
Let's take a look:

1. Keep Learning. OK, this is obvious. You gotta keep learning. Keep seeking knowledge, keep asking questions, keep LEARNING.
2. Quiz yourself. If you're serious about this subject, you need to quiz yourself. First review your knowledge, and get someone to ask questions. You could also take on some online quizzes.
3. Maps, maps, maps. Find coutries on a map, and .learn just 1 fact about 20-30 countries. This can go a long way.
4. Try to understand each concept. For example, not like this: "Pakistan has a population of 225,199,937 eactly.', but like, "Pakistan has about 225,000000 population."
5. For land masses, there's not much to do. I mean, their are just a few. Learn some of their names, and how they are formed. That's about it.

Why learn geography?
